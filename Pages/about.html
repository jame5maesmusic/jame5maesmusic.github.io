James Wicks

Mixer | Mastering Engineer | Producer | Composer | Percussionist

Hello! I’m James, a Berklee graduate and an audio engineer and producer with 5 years of experience in the industry. When I work on a project my mission is to find the core of the music, what made someone write it, and bring it to life with my own skills and talents. I especially enjoy adding small, subtle details, like candid moments from unused takes, that bring the music to life even more.

I hear from clients often that working with me is the first time they’ve felt like they could fully be themselves while creating, which is something I’m very proud of. If my clients feel like they can be themselves, then they’ll make more personal and meaningful music.

I work primarily on rock, pop, and RnB projects, but I’m comfortable in all sorts of other genres. I also have experience recording, mixing, and mastering for orchestras.

Feel free to reach out to see how we can work together to make your music great!
